## Things you may want to check yourself

- **Fn 4**: your sentence also mentions accepting A's authority "in some other act of recognition". Pp.23/31 cover the submission and the *acceptance* of a command. Reinach only talks about *recognition* (*Anerkennung*) on p.117, and there it's about the efficacy of legal enactments, not orders. I didn't add p.117. Add it if that's the passage you meant.
- **Fn 22 / 24 / 29 / 30 / 32**: your main text quotes the German terms (e.g. "eigentliche Passivität des Ich", "Unterwerfungsverhältnis", "beugt sich"). The 2012 edition is Crosby's English translation; it gives some German terms in brackets but not all of them. I didn't change your main text. Only the page numbers now point to the English edition.
- **Bibliography**: the paper has no bibliography yet, so there was no Reinach entry to update.
